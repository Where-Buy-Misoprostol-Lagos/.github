## What each gap costs you

- **claimed:** an unclaimed profile can be edited by anyone, and you cannot reply to reviews.
- **website:** patients who want to check services or prices before calling have nowhere to go.
- **description / hours:** Google has less to match you against searches like "clinic open now near me".
- **photos:** listings with no photos of the building or staff look closed or untrustworthy.
- **review_count / rating:** most patients choose between the first few clinics by reviews alone.

reviews 50+ = 20, 20-49 = 15, 5-19 = 10, 1-4 = 5; rating 4.5+ = 10, 4.0-4.4 = 7, 3.5-3.9 = 4.
